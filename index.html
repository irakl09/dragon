Print tips

Clean
results

No supports

Designed print-in-place. Disable supports. On some beds a tiny brim helps adhesion of the first layer.

Layer height

0.16–0.20 mm works best for smooth joint movement. Too thick can fuse segments.

Infill

5% is enough for multi-color; 0–5% for single color keeps weight low and joints free.

First play

Gently work each joint a few times after printing. They free up quickly and stay smooth.
